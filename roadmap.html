Roadmap

The build plan

Three steps, in order. Each one unlocks a clear decision for owners.

  1. 1

    You are here

    Trust what the gauges say

    • Make every owner gauge clickable.
    • Open a side panel that shows the real quotes, jobs, accounts, or records behind the colour.
    • See the source, how fresh the data is, the rule that set the colour, who owns it, and any overrides.
    • We start with a side panel — a popup only if you must decide something and return straight away.

    Decision this unlocks Click through to the real records behind a colour.

  2. 2

    Next

    See if we have enough people

    • Show demand versus available hours by trade or specialty.
    • Look ahead in the windows owners already use: now, 0–3 months, 3–6 months, and 6–12 months.
    • Answer one question: do we hire, move people, or protect capacity?
    • Keep the labels clear and provisional until planned demand, estimates, available hours, and hiring lead time are proven.

    Decision this unlocks Hire, redeploy, or protect capacity.

  3. 3

    Later

    Screen built for decisions

    • Keep the time-first reading order you already know.
    • Rearrange the layout around the few decisions an owner must make — not around data piles.
    • Every visible gauge must answer four things: What is happening? Why this colour? What decision is expected? Who owns the next move?

    Decision this unlocks Every gauge answers what / why / decide / who.
